<a id="MEM-AUD-001"></a>
### [MEM-AUD-001] 12-State Application Lifecycle FSM `[PROPOSED]`
State machine consists of 12 validated states:
`DRAFT` → `SUBMITTED` → `UNDER_REVIEW` → `ACTION_REQUIRED` → `APPROVED` → `PAYMENT_PENDING` → `PAYMENT_CONFIRMED` → `ENROLLED` → `COMPLETED`, plus branch states `WAITLISTED`, `REJECTED`, and `CANCELLED`.
- **WAITLISTED State Integrity:** `WAITLISTED` is an active non-terminal holding state. It can transition to `APPROVED` (when seats open) or `CANCELLED`. It is never a terminal error state.
- **Audit Requirement:** Every state change must record `actor_id`, `actor_role`, `previous_status`, `new_status`, `change_reason`, and `timestamp` in `application_audit_logs`.

## 6. Detailed Analysis of Open Client Decisions

1. **DEC-017: Core MVP Boundary (Option A vs Option B):**
   - *Option A (Extended MVP - Phases 1 to 10):* Delivers a complete end-to-end credentialing lifecycle including Attendance Tracking, Assessment Records, and QR-verifiable PDF Certificates in the initial launch. Requires full operational readiness of trainer grading workflows before going live.
   - *Option B (Lean MVP - Phases 1 to 8):* Launches public marketing, course discovery, admissions, payment collection, and admin batch scheduling immediately. Defers Attendance and QR Certificates to Phase 9–10. Prevents delays in student enrollment while testing grading policies.
   - *Blocking Impact:* Governs acceptance criteria for production launch gating.

2. **DEC-015: Payment Gateway Selection:**
   - Requires registered Indian corporate legal entity credentials, PAN, GSTIN, and active bank account for Razorpay/Cashfree KYC.
   - *Blocking Impact:* Blocks production merchant keys for Phase 6 checkout. Offline payment recording (`OFFLINE_BANK_TRANSFER`) is provided as an architectural fallback.

3. **DEC-018: Institutional Cohort Ceilings:**
   - Clarifies whether flat institutional training rates (₹15,000 for schools, ₹25,000 for colleges) cover batches up to 50, 100, or unlimited students.
   - *Blocking Impact:* Impacts inquiry terms and trainer dispatch staffing formulas.

4. **DEC-019: Student Identity Proof Upload:**
   - Determines whether government/student ID upload is mandatory during online admission or deferred to in-person physical registration.
   - *Blocking Impact:* Governs frontend form validation and S3 private document upload requirements in Phase 4.

5. **DEC-020: Physical Venue & Training Center Addresses:**
   - Requires formal physical street address in Pune and affiliated farm partner locations.
   - *Blocking Impact:* Blocks final contact page and brochure PDF display text.

6. **DEC-021: Telecom DLT SMS/WhatsApp Provider:**
   - Requires registration with Indian telecommunications regulatory DLT portal (e.g., Jio, Airtel, Vodafone) for commercial SMS/WhatsApp transactional templates.
   - *Blocking Impact:* Blocks automated mobile OTP delivery in Phase 7; email notifications serve as primary fallback.

7. **DEC-022: Entity Legal Structure & Authorized Signatories:**
   - Requires confirmation of Section 8 / Trust registration number, official seal, and digital signatures of the Founder/Managing Trustee for certificates.
   - *Blocking Impact:* Blocks Phase 10 PDF certificate template finalization.

8. **DEC-023: Fee Taxation & GST Registration:**
   - Requires confirmation of whether training fees are exempt under non-profit educational provisions or subject to 18% GST.
   - *Blocking Impact:* Governs invoice generation, tax breakdown display, and fee calculation logic in Phase 6.
